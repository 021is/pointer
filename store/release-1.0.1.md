## What's new in 1.0.1

- **Your marks stay when the page reloads.** Open Pointer again and they're back, style changes too. If something you marked has left the page, Pointer tells you which one.
- **The shortcut works on every computer.** It's Alt+P, and Control+Shift+P on a Mac (that one types no character). Hold the mouse over Pointer's icon to see your key. If Chrome gave Pointer no key, a button helps you set one.
- **Redo.** ⌘⇧Z on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y elsewhere. It also brings back a Clear everything.
- **A shorter bar.** The tools sit in groups, the drawing tools share one button (▾ for the others), and Copy is smaller.
- **Idea (Q).** Mark something that could be better. Your agent answers like a designer: what's wrong and why, 2–3 fixes, and it asks before building.
- **Gap (G).** Drag from one element to another and Pointer measures the space between them.
- **Text on a box or an ellipse.** Double-click it and type.
- **Icons.** Search Lucide's icons, then click one or drag it onto the page.
- A pasted image switches to Move, so you can drag it right away.
- Styles always answers: it opens, or it tells you why it can't.

New permission: `storage`. It keeps your marks in Chrome's memory, for that tab only, until you clear them, close Pointer or close the tab. Pointer still sends nothing. [Privacy](https://021.is/plugins/pointer/privacy)
