## What's new in 1.1.0

- **Works on a phone-sized page.** In Chrome DevTools' device mode, or any narrow window, the bar docks to the bottom of the page, each tool named under its icon, and fits on a 320 px phone: Pick, Comment, Draw, Undo and Copy stay one tap away, and a tablet-sized page shows more. **More** opens a sheet with the rest in three groups, Mark, Edit and Send, the page still in view above it; tap Close, choose a tool, tap outside or drag it down to close it. The colours show there while you draw. The menus, the note box, the style box and the icon picker all fit the screen.
- **Touch works.** Tap to comment or pick, and drag with a finger to draw, crop, measure a gap or move marks and pasted images, the way DevTools' device mode sends them.
- **Icons land where you click.** Choose the Icon tool (L), click a spot, and pick one of five quick icons, or More to search them all. Before, an icon could land under the icon list and was hard to move. An icon now also moves when you drag its number.
- **Crops you scrolled away still make it into the picture.** Copy and Preview scroll to each crop, take it, and put the page back exactly where you left it. A crop larger than the screen shows its top part, and Pointer tells you how to get all of it.
- Your agent is told the size of the page you marked ("viewport 390×844"), so it knows which breakpoint you mean.

Also in this release (1.0.1 was not published on the store): your marks stay when the page reloads; the shortcut works on every computer (Alt+P, Control+Shift+P on a Mac); Redo; a shorter bar; Idea (Q); Gap (G); text on a box or an ellipse; icons; a pasted image switches to Move; Styles always answers.

Storage: Pointer keeps your marks in Chrome's session memory, for that tab only, until you clear them, close Pointer or close the tab. Pointer still sends nothing. [Privacy](https://021.is/plugins/pointer/privacy)
