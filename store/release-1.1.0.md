## What's new in 1.1.0

- **See your page on a phone or a tablet.** The new Screen button on the bar shows your page at Tablet (768 × 1024), Mobile (390 × 844) or a size you type, inside a simple device frame. Mark it there as usual: your agent is told which size the marks are about ("viewport 390×844 (Mobile)"). Full brings the page back as it was. Your custom size is remembered.
- **Icons land where you click.** Choose the Icon tool (L), click a spot, and pick one of five quick icons, or More to search them all. Before, an icon could land under the icon list and was hard to move. An icon now also moves when you drag its number.
- A few sites don't let themselves be shown inside a frame. Pointer says so in one sentence and stays on Full.

Also in this release (1.0.1 was not published on the store): your marks stay when the page reloads; the shortcut works on every computer (Alt+P, Control+Shift+P on a Mac); Redo; a shorter bar; Idea (Q); Gap (G); text on a box or an ellipse; icons; a pasted image switches to Move; Styles always answers.

Storage: Pointer keeps your marks in Chrome's session memory, for that tab only, until you clear them, close Pointer or close the tab. New in 1.1.0, it remembers one setting on your computer: the custom screen size you typed (two numbers). Pointer still sends nothing. [Privacy](https://021.is/plugins/pointer/privacy)
