Pointer is ready.

Show your coding agent exactly what to change: point at the page, then paste one picture with the source file:line of every element you marked into Claude Code, Cursor or any AI chat.

Start

  1. Open the page you are working on (a dev build gives the file and line).
  2. Press Alt+Shift+P, or click Pointer's icon in the toolbar. Pin it from the puzzle menu to keep it there.
  3. Pick an element, comment, crop or draw. Then Copy and paste into your agent's chat.

Keys

PPick an element. ↑ ↓ walk to its parent or child, Enter takes it, then a note.
CComment: click anywhere, type, Enter. Click a number to edit it.
XCrop a component (click) or a frame (drag). Several crops make one picture.
D A B E TPen, arrow, box, ellipse, text. Hold Shift for straight lines and squares.
SStyles of the last pick: change its CSS live; the changes go into the copy.
⌘V / Ctrl+VPaste a screenshot or a mockup onto the page. [ ] make it see-through.
IPicture or Text: Text copies only the lines, about 3× cheaper when your words say enough.
⌘C / Ctrl+CCopy for the agent. Esc twice closes Pointer.

The file and line come from your app's dev build (React, Vue, Svelte). On any other page you still get the element, its text and its styles.

Privacy

Pointer runs only on the tab where you turn it on, and only until you turn it off. Nothing leaves your browser: what you mark goes to your clipboard, and you decide where to paste it.
