Pointer is ready.

Show your coding agent exactly what to change. Point at the page, then paste one picture with the source file:line of every element you marked into Claude Code, Cursor or any AI chat.

Start

  1. Open the page you are working on. A dev build gives the file and the line.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+P, or click Pointer's icon. Pin it from the puzzle menu to keep it in the toolbar.
  3. Comment, pick, crop or draw. Then Copy and paste into your agent's chat.

Keys

CComment: click anywhere, type, Enter. Click a number to edit it.
PPick an element. ↑ ↓ walk to its parent or child; Enter takes it.
XCrop a component (click) or a frame (drag). Several crops make one picture.
D A B E TPen, arrow, box, ellipse, text. Shift for straight lines and squares.
MMove marks and pasted images. Delete removes the one under the mouse.
SStyles of the last pick: change its CSS live; the changes go into the copy.
⌘V Ctrl+VPaste a screenshot or a mockup onto the page. [ ] make it see-through.
⌘C Ctrl+CCopy. ▾ next to Copy: picture + text, text only or picture only, each with its cost, and a preview.
Esc EscClose Pointer.

The file and line come from your app's dev build (React, Vue, Svelte). On any other page you still get the element, its text and its styles.

Privacy

Pointer runs only on the tab where you turn it on, and only until you turn it off. It stores nothing and sends nothing: what you mark goes to your clipboard, and you decide where to paste it. The privacy policy.
